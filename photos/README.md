# Photographing the vehicle

Use descriptive filenames such as `2026-09-10_carburettor_left_side_01.jpg`.

For every assembly, capture:

- Overall location in the vehicle
- Front, rear, left, right, top, and accessible underside
- Connections before disassembly
- Fasteners and brackets
- Identification markings
- A ruler or known-size reference when geometry matters

Do not publish photographs containing the VIN, registration documents, home address, or other private identifiers.
