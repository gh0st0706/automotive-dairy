# My Maruti 800 Engineering Project

## Mission

Document, measure, and explain every major subsystem in the Maruti 800. Connect mechanical operation, symptoms, measurements, photographs, diagrams, and the 3D model.

## Questions for each component

1. What does it do?
2. What principle makes it work?
3. What enters and leaves it?
4. How does it interact with other parts?
5. What happens when it fails?
6. How can it be tested safely?

## Vehicle baseline

- Vehicle: Maruti Suzuki 800
- Engine family: F8B, carburetted inline-three
- Age: approx. 26 years
- Variant: Type 2
- Transmission: Manual; verify exact spec
- Odometer: record
- VIN/chassis: private
- Engine number: private

## Observations to verify

- Cold idle rough; occasional stalling; smoother after warm-up
- Mechanic noted missing/misfiring
- Accelerator cable previously stuck; replaced
- Vehicle previously ran out of fuel
- Fuel-gauge response questionable
- Exhaust showed rhythmic water vapour and dark residue

These are observations, not diagnoses. Each must be reproduced, measured, or marked resolved.

## Outputs

- Labelled 3D model
- Subsystem map
- Component photographs
- Inspection and measurement log
- Fault–symptom map
- Wiring and hose-routing sketches
- Short technical notes for each subsystem
- Final report: **How My Maruti 800 Works**

## Working method

For each subsystem:

1. Read service information.
2. Locate parts on the car.
3. Photograph the assembly first.
4. Trace connections.
5. Record measurements with units and conditions.
6. Compare with expected behaviour.
7. Update the model and notes.
8. Separate facts, measurements, and hypotheses.

## Safety boundary

- Disconnect battery before exposed electrical work.
- Never work under a vehicle supported only by a jack.
- Keep hands, tools, and wires away from belts and fans while running.
- Do not open the cooling system while hot.
- Work with petrol only in ventilated areas, away from sparks and flames.
- Do not probe ignition high-voltage wiring by hand.
- Record first; dismantle only when the procedure and reassembly are clear.

**Fuel tank → fuel pump → carburettor → intake manifold → intake valve → cylinder → piston → connecting rod → crankshaft → flywheel → clutch → gearbox → differential → driveshafts → front wheels**

Completion:
