# My Maruti 800 Engineering Project

## Mission

Document, understand, measure, and explain every major subsystem in my Maruti 800. The finished project should connect mechanical operation, real symptoms, measurements, photographs, diagrams, and the 3D vehicle model.

This is an engineering study—not merely a restoration diary. Every entry should answer:

1. What does the component do?
2. What physical principle makes it work?
3. What enters and leaves it?
4. How does it interact with other components?
5. What happens when it fails?
6. How can I test it safely?

## Working method

For each subsystem:

1. Read the relevant service information.
2. Locate the components on the real car.
3. Photograph the assembly before touching anything.
4. Trace mechanical, electrical, air, fuel, oil, or coolant connections.
5. Record measurements with units and operating conditions.
6. Compare observations with the expected behaviour.
7. Update the 3D model and subsystem notes.
8. Clearly separate confirmed facts, measurements, and hypotheses.

## Safety boundary

- Disconnect the battery before exposed electrical work where appropriate.
- Never work underneath a vehicle supported only by a jack.
- Keep hands, clothing, tools, and wires away from belts and fans while running.
- Do not open the cooling system while hot.
- Work with petrol only in a ventilated area, away from sparks and flames.
- Do not probe ignition high-voltage wiring by hand.
- Record first; dismantle only when the procedure and reassembly requirements are understood.
